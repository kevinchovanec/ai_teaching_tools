Roles and discussion questions

Roles: timekeeper, recorder, and stakeholder voice (the person who speaks as the person on the card).

  1. Whose project is most affected by this card? Work the card through that project.
  2. What is legitimate or accurate about this concern?
  3. How would this person encounter the project, and what would they see?
  4. What would you say to them in two or three plain sentences?
  5. What, if anything, would you change about the project?
  6. If the card does not apply to your project, explain why in one sentence.
