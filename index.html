AI Teaching Tools

A collection of small, interactive tools and workshop activities for teaching AI/ML concepts. Everything here runs in your browser — no accounts, no servers, nothing installed.

Topics

Embeddings — Embedding Explorer

Type words or paragraphs and see how a language model represents meaning as vectors — analogies, similarity, and a 2D map of embedding space. A walkthrough notebook covering the same ground is also in the repo, under embeddings/.

Attention — Attention Lab

Work through scaled dot-product attention with the real math: compute it by hand, see why it is scaled and masked, trace weights inside GPT-2, and test what they do and don't explain. For learners comfortable with matrix multiplication.

Workshops

Tinker Group Cards

A live small-group discussion activity for AI-adoption workshops — roll for a stakeholder-concern card, work it through with assigned roles, then draw a twist partway through.
